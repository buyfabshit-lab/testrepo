Swell

The mark is never drawn. The wave swells, the crest breaks into whitewash, and the sheet runs in over everything — and then it leaves. Most of that water drains back out to sea; what the mark holds stays behind. The letters are what the water stopped covering, which is how a wave actually leaves a shape on a beach: by subtraction, not by drawing. Click the stage and an offshore wind takes what is standing, all but the droplets that carry on into the corner and set the mark up again, small, where a site header would hold it. Behind it the surface is long jagged strands, straight run to straight run, kinking slightly at every vertex — the bends carry the ripple and nothing is ever curved.
