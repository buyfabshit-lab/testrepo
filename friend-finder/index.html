How this works

Instagram shows you riders. This board tells you which ones to follow.

No bots, no auto-follow. You work the hashtag feeds by hand, score each rider against seven signs that they will follow back and engage, and log the keepers to your crew list. Twenty minutes a day beats any follow app, and your account stays clean.

Playbook

What actually grows a Harley page.

Follow-backs get you the first thousand. After that it is the content and the crew that carry you. Keep both running.

Content pillars

  • The bike, lit right. Golden hour, three-quarter front, low angle. Chrome needs a clean background.
  • Wrench night. Hands, tools, parts on the bench. Progress shots get saved and shared.
  • The ride. Helmet-cam clips under 15 seconds, road and exhaust note, no music over the pipes.
  • The culture. Patches, vests, bike night, rally crowds. Tag the people in the shot.
  • The story. Why you ride, who taught you, the first bike. One honest post a week outperforms five bike photos.

Posting rhythm

  • Reels 3 to 4 a week, posted 6 to 8 pm local or Saturday morning when riders are off the bike and on the phone.
  • Stories daily. Polls ("Bagger or Dyna?"), ride-day countdowns, reposts of crew members' bikes.
  • Carousel once a week. Build threads, before and after, parts lists. Carousels get the longest watch time.
  • Reply to every comment within the hour. The first hour decides reach.
  • Caption hook first. Lead with the question or the number: "Four hours, one stripped bolt." Tags at the end or in the first comment.

Comment scripts

Edit before you post. A copied comment reads as a bot.

Clean stance on that Street Glide. What bars are those?
That Shovel sounds mean. Stock cam or did you go up?
Love the patina on the tank. Original paint?
Where was this shot? Looks like a road I need to ride.
Bike night this week? We roll out of the east side around 7.

DM openers for follow-backs

Send one to each new follow-back. No links, no asks.

Appreciate the follow. What are you riding right now?
Saw your Road Glide in the baggernation feed. Where do you ride out of?
Your build thread is solid. Mind if I share your bike in my story and tag you?
Any rides or bike nights near you coming up? Always down to meet riders on the road.

Crew tactics

  • Share their bike in your story and tag them. Most will share it back to their followers.
  • Engagement pods of 5 to 8 riders. A group DM where everyone comments on each other's posts in the first hour.
  • Collab posts. Instagram's Collab feature puts one post in both feeds. Ask crew members with 1k to 10k followers.
  • Rally and bike night coverage. Shoot other people's bikes, post them, tag them. Every tagged rider is a new follower and a share.
  • Local first. A hundred riders in your county are worth more than a thousand across the world. They show up, they share, they bring friends.

What gets accounts killed

  • Follow apps and bots. Instagram detects them within days. Shadow restriction first, ban second.
  • Buying followers. Dead accounts crater your reach, because Instagram sees nobody engaging.
  • Mass follow then mass unfollow. Churn is the clearest bot signal there is.
  • Copy-paste comments. "Nice bike 🔥" twenty times gets flagged as spam and reads as spam.
  • Engagement bait. "Follow for follow" and "like if you ride" posts get downranked.
